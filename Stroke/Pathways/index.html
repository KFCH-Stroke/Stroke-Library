Stroke Alert Workflow

Suspected acute stroke → Stroke Alert activation → immediate neurological assessment/NIHSS → point-of-care glucose and essential history → non-contrast CT → CTA when indicated → determine IV thrombolysis eligibility and LVO status → treatment/EVT pathway → stroke unit or ICU disposition.

IV Thrombolysis Pathway

Confirm time last known well and eligibility → obtain brain imaging → exclude hemorrhage and major contraindications → establish weight-based thrombolytic plan → administer eligible thrombolytic promptly → frequent neurological and blood-pressure monitoring → manage complications → transfer to appropriate monitored stroke care.

Mechanical Thrombectomy Pathway

Identify suspected LVO → CTA/MRA or appropriate vascular imaging → assess clinical and imaging eligibility → activate neurointerventional team → anesthesia/airway assessment when required → transfer to angiography suite → EVT → reperfusion assessment → post-procedure neurological and access-site monitoring.

ICH Pathway

Immediate stabilization → urgent non-contrast head CT → blood-pressure assessment and controlled reduction when indicated → identify anticoagulant/antithrombotic exposure and initiate appropriate reversal when indicated → assess hydrocephalus, mass effect and herniation risk → neurosurgical/critical-care consultation → monitored ICU-level care.

SAH Pathway

Recognize suspected SAH → urgent brain imaging → vascular imaging when indicated → immediate neurosurgical/neurocritical-care consultation → manage blood pressure and physiological complications → assess hydrocephalus and need for CSF diversion → definitive aneurysm treatment pathway → neurocritical care monitoring and complication prevention.

LVO Transfer Pathway

Identify potential LVO at referring facility → initiate local stroke assessment and imaging → determine IV thrombolysis eligibility without unnecessary delay → activate receiving thrombectomy-capable center → send imaging and clinical information → minimize door-in-door-out time → pre-arrival receiving-team activation → direct transfer to definitive treatment.

Governance note

These pathways are an operational library framework. Final clinical pathways should be approved through KFCH Stroke Program governance and document control before clinical implementation.
